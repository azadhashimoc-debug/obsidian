# Product Builder & Founder Strategy (product-founder-advisor)

Strategic framework for scoping, validating, launching, and monetizing digital software products with solo-founder efficiency.

---

## 1. When to Use & Triggers
- Brainstorming, refining, or scoping a new digital product / SaaS idea.
- Defining MVP (Minimum Viable Product) features vs Post-launch backlog.
- Designing pricing tiers, freemium mechanics, and monetization models.
- Analyzing competitor products, market gaps, and customer objections.
- Optimizing user activation and retention funnels.

---

## 2. What TO Do (Core Principles)
- **The Lean MVP Rule:** Ruthlessly eliminate non-essential features until the product solves one problem 10x better.
- **Speed to Value:** Time from sign-up to 'Aha! moment' must be under 60 seconds.
- **Pricing & Risk-Reversal:** Compete on speed and user experience; offer transparent pricing with clear guarantees.

---

## 3. What NOT To Do
- ❌ Do NOT build speculative features without validating demand with real users.
- ❌ Do NOT hide pricing or make the checkout flow complicated.

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Workflow
1. **Problem Definition:** Articulate the exact pain point and target audience.
2. **Competitor Teardown:** Analyze competitor pricing, strengths, and user complaints.
3. **Scope Lean MVP:** List 3 core features required for launch; backlog everything else.
4. **Define Pricing Model:** Choose monetization (Subscription, One-time, Usage/Balance-based).
5. **Plan Launch & Acquisition:** Define channels (Meta ads, organic SEO, communities).

---

## 5. Production Checklist
- [ ] Is the core value proposition defined in one concise sentence?
- [ ] Is the MVP scope locked with strictly essential features?
- [ ] Is the pricing model and payment integration verified?
- [ ] Is the onboarding flow frictionless (minimal required form fields)?
