# Conversion Copywriting & Funnel Strategy (conversion-copywriter)

Mastery of direct-response copywriting, customer psychology, hook formulation, and conversion optimization for digital products and SaaS.

---

## 1. When to Use & Triggers
- Writing landing page hero sections, value propositions, and feature breakdowns.
- Creating high-ROI advertising copy (Meta Ads, TikTok, Google Search).
- Crafting TikTok/Reels video scripts with 3-second pattern interrupts.
- Designing high-converting pricing tables, risk-reversal offers, and FAQs.

---

## 2. What TO Do (Core Principles)
- **PAS Framework:** Clearly state the Problem, Agitate the consequences of inaction, and present the Solution.
- **Irresistible Offers:** Include risk-reversal (guarantees, free trial balance, transparent pricing).
- **Above the Fold Clarity:** 5-second rule — visitor must immediately know what the product is and how to get it.
- **Benefit-Driven Bullets:** State the feature, then attach the tangible user benefit.

---

## 3. What NOT To Do
- ❌ Do NOT use vague corporate jargon.
- ❌ Do NOT bury the primary call to action below deep walls of unformatted text.
- ❌ Do NOT talk only about features without addressing customer pain points.

---

## 4. Step-by-Step Workflow
1. **Target Persona Research:** Identify core desires, pain points, and objections.
2. **Draft Hero Headline:** Combine desired outcome + speed - friction.
3. **Structure Value Proposition:** Write 3-4 feature cards with benefit statements.
4. **Craft Social Proof & Offer:** Add testimonials, user stats, and clear risk-free CTA.
5. **Review & Trim:** Remove fluff and shorten sentences for fast scanning.

---

## 5. Production Checklist
- [ ] Does the main headline pass the 5-second clarity test?
- [ ] Is the primary CTA visible above the fold on mobile and desktop?
- [ ] Are risk reversals (guarantees, support) clearly stated?
- [ ] Are social proof elements placed near CTAs?
